The Math Behind Flexible Macro Dieting

Understand the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, goal adjustments, lean-body-mass protein targets, and how carbs fill the remainder.

How The Formula Works

First, Basal Metabolic Rate is estimated with Mifflin-St Jeor: for men BMR = 10 × weight(kg) + 6.25 × height(cm) − 5 × age + 5; for women the constant is − 161. TDEE multiplies BMR by an activity factor (1.2–1.9). A goal adjustment then sets the calorie target: fat loss × 0.80, maintain × 1.00, or lean gain × 1.15.

Macro & Calorie Splitting

Protein carries 4 kcal/g, fat 9 kcal/g, and carbs 4 kcal/g. Protein is set at 1.6–2.2 g per kilogram of lean body mass (body weight minus body fat, estimated with the Deurenberg formula if not entered). Fat takes 25–35% of calories. Carbs = (target kcal − protein kcal − fat kcal) ÷ 4 fills the remainder. The meal-split table divides each gram target evenly across 3, 4, or 5 meals.

Troubleshooting & Edge-Case Failure Points

  • Unit conversion: pounds are divided by 2.20462 to become kilograms. Entering 75 in the pound field while thinking "kg" silently adds ~59 kg of mass to the equation.
  • Estimated body fat: the Deurenberg formula (1.2 × BMI + 0.23 × age − 10.8 × sex − 5.4) is a population estimate; very lean or very muscular individuals should enter a measured body-fat value for a more precise protein target.
  • Very low-carb check: if fat plus protein already exceeds the calorie target, carbohydrate grams go to zero and the tool warns instead of showing a negative number.
  • Activity overstatement: most people choose one tier too high. A "moderately active" desk worker who trains twice a week often overshoots by 300+ kcal/day.
  • Recalculation: after a 5–10 kg (10–20 lb) weight change, both BMR and lean mass shift enough to move targets — recompute the plan.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Select your biological sex — it chooses the +5 or −161 constant in the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
  2. Enter your age, weight (kg or lb), and height in centimetres.
  3. Pick an honest activity level and your calorie goal (fat loss, maintain, or lean gain).
  4. Optionally enter a measured body-fat percentage, or leave it blank for the automatic BMI-based estimate.
  5. Set your protein target (1.6–2.2 g/kg lean mass) and fat target (25–35% of calories); carbs fill the remainder automatically.
  6. Choose 3–5 meals, press "Calculate Macros", then copy your daily and per-meal targets.
