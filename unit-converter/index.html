Core Use Case

Unit conversion is essential in engineering, science, cooking, and daily life. This tool handles length, weight, volume, temperature, area, and time conversions instantly.

Troubleshooting & Edge Cases

  • Temperature conversions require offset adjustments (°C ↔ °F).
  • Very large or very small numbers may show scientific notation.
  • All conversions are approximate for imperial units.
