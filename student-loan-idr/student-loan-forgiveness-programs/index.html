Student Loan Forgiveness Programs & IDR Forgiveness

Use the Student Loan IDR Engine on the /student-loan-idr page to model your total paid, forgiven balance, and timeline under SAVE, IBR, and PAYE. Understanding these projections helps you decide whether IDR forgiveness alone is sufficient or whether you should also pursue PSLF or another discharge option.

How to Apply for Forgiveness

  1. Confirm your loan type and servicer. Direct Loans are required for most forgiveness programs.
  2. Enroll in an IDR plan and begin making qualifying payments.
  3. Submit annual recertification to maintain IDR eligibility and protect your forgiveness timeline.
  4. For PSLF, submit the Employment Certification Form each year to document qualifying employment.
  5. After reaching the required number of payments, submit a forgiveness application through your servicer or the Federal Student Aid website.
  6. Review your credit and tax records after forgiveness to ensure the discharged balance is reported correctly.

Major Forgiveness and Discharge Programs

Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) forgives the remaining balance after 120 qualifying payments while working full time for a qualifying employer. Income-Driven Repayment forgiveness discharges whatever balance remains after 20 or 25 years of qualifying IDR payments, though the forgiven amount may be taxable depending on your state. Teacher Loan Forgiveness cancels up to $17,500 for highly qualified teachers who work five consecutive years in a low-income school, independent of IDR forgiveness.

Borrower Defense and Discharge Options

Beyond forgiveness, borrowers may qualify for a total and permanent disability discharge, a discharge after a school's closure, or borrower defense to repayment if a school misled them. These options do not require years of payments, but they do require documentation. Running your numbers through the Student Loan IDR Engine first helps you decide whether waiting out an IDR forgiveness timeline beats pursuing a discharge today. Whatever program you pursue, keep copies of every form you submit and your servicer's responses, since approval often depends on a complete paper trail.
