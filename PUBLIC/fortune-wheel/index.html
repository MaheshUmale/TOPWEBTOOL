Core Use Case scenario

Teachers, coordinators, and decision-makers often need an unbiased, interactive tool to make random selections. Whether choosing classroom names, picking giveaway winners, or resolving everyday choices, an interactive canvas spinner makes decision-making fun and fair.

Troubleshooting & Edge-Case Failure Points

  • Canvas rendering delays: If you add more than 100 choices, the labels can overlap and look messy; keep lists under 50 choices for the best visual experience.
  • Deterministic results: To ensure true fairness, selections use cryptographically random numbers rather than simple math timers.
  • Saving custom configurations: If your browser cookies are cleared, custom lists may reset to default values. Save important lists in a text file to reuse them easily.

Detailed Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Input your list of choices, names, or alternatives into the data entry panel, or select the Yes/No preset.
  2. Adjust visual styles, such as segment colors, sound effects, and spin durations.
  3. Click the center of the wheel or spin button to start the animation and let the wheel spin.
  4. Review the selected choice, then choose to keep it in the list or remove it to prevent repeat wins.
