Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI) is a safeguard required by lenders when a home buyer puts down less than 20% of the home's purchase price. PMI protects the lender, not the borrower, in case of a default. Although it allows buyers to purchase homes sooner with smaller down payments, it adds a substantial recurring cost.
