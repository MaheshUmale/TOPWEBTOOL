Where the Gibberish Comes From

The text traces to De Finibus Bonorum et Malorum, Cicero's 45 BC treatise on ethics — "Neque porro quisquam est qui dole­m ipsum…" — scrambled into printing-industry filler by a typesetter (the tradition is traced to Aldus Manutius's 1500s workshop) and popularized when Letraset transfer sheets carried it into 1960s design studios, then into desktop publishing defaults. Why nonsense beats realistic filler: placeholder text must be boring by design — real-looking content invites clients to proofread the layout instead of judging the layout, which is the entire point of a mockup. Modern alternates ("space ipsum", corporate-lorem variants) exist for when stakeholders need genre-appropriate flavor.

Why do designers use lorem ipsum?

What language is lorem ipsum?

When should I avoid placeholder text?
