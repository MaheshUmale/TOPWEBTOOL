Tic Tac Toe

Classic 3x3 strategy game. Play against a friend or challenge the AI.

How to Play: Objective: Be the first to get 3 of your marks (X or O) in a row, column, or diagonal. Controls: Click a cell to place your mark, or use Tab + Enter. Press R to restart.
