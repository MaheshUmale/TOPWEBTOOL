Wordle — How to Play & Choose Better Start Words

Goal: guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries. After each guess, letters turn green (right letter, right spot), yellow (right letter, wrong spot), or gray (not in the word).

Three strategy tips

  • Open with vowel-heavy words. Words like ARISE, SLATE, or CRANE reveal letter positions fast.
  • Second guess should test new letters. Even with greens, spend guess two eliminating five more consonants.
  • Watch letter frequency. In five-letter English words, the most common letters are roughly E, A, R, O, T, L, I, S, N — prioritize them.

FAQ

Can letters repeat in the answer?

Yes — a green E does not mean there is only one E; test repeats when clues allow.

What is the mathematically best start word?

Information-theory analyses consistently favor words like SALET, CRANE, and SLATE; any of them is excellent.

Gray letter already used — now what?

Exclude it from every future guess; keeping a mental (or written) elimination list is the fastest way to improve.
