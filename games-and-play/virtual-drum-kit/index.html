Virtual Drum Kit

8-pad drum kit with synthesized sounds. Record loops and build beats with your keyboard.

How to Play: Objective: Create beats by hitting drum pads. Controls: Click/tap pads or use keys A S D F G H J K. Record and loop your beat.

Keys: A = Kick • S = Snare • D = Hi-Hat Closed • F = Hi-Hat Open • G = Tom High • H = Tom Mid • J = Tom Low • K = Crash
