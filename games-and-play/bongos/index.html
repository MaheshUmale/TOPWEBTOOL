Bongos

Hit the high and low drums. Lay down a rhythm with your keyboard or mouse.

How to Play: Hit the high drum (A key or click left drum) for a sharp slap. Hit the low drum (S key or click right drum) for a deep thump. Click the edge of a drum for a rimshot. Adjust volume with the slider below.
