Online Piano

2-octave playable piano with mouse, touch, and keyboard. Record and playback your melodies.

How to Play: Objective: Play music by clicking/tapping keys or using your keyboard. Controls: Mouse/Touch to play notes, Keyboard keys A-L for white keys, W-U for black keys. Space = sustain toggle, Z/X = octave shift.

REC
Use keyboard keys A S D F G H J K L ; to play white keys • W E T Y U O P for black keys • Space = sustain • Z/X = octave down/up
