Breakout — How to Play & Break More Bricks

Goal: bounce the ball with your paddle to destroy every brick at the top of the screen. Lose the ball three times and the run ends.

Three technique tips

  • Aim with the paddle edge. Hitting the ball with the side of the paddle sends it at a sharp angle — the fastest way to reach bricks deep in the corners.
  • Clear a channel early. Opening a gap on one side lets the ball get above the brick field and bounce along the top, clearing rows automatically.
  • Stay center-late. As bricks thin out, play from the middle: it minimizes travel distance to any bounce.

FAQ

Why does the ball speed up?

Classic Breakout raises ball speed each time you hit the top wall or clear a threshold of bricks — early angles are cheap, late angles are lethal.

What is a good score?

Clearing the first screen with 2 lives in reserve is a solid benchmark for casual play.
