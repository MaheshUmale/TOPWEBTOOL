Whack A Mole Guide

Whack A Mole is a fast-paced arcade game built directly into your browser. Moles pop up randomly across a 3x3 grid — click or tap them before they disappear. Every consecutive hit raises your combo multiplier, but a miss resets your streak.

How to Play

  • Press Play to start a 20-second round.
  • Click or tap a mole when it appears to score points.
  • Consecutive hits build a combo: x2, x3, then x5.
  • Clicking an empty hole resets your combo to x1.
  • After 10 points, the game speeds up.

Combo Strategy

Prioritize accuracy over speed. A missed tap not only loses a mole but also drops your multiplier back to x1. As the level increases, moles stay up for shorter windows, so focus on the center holes first where your cursor travel is shortest.

Difficulty Ramp

Every 10 points, mole up-time and intervals decrease. By level 3 or 4 the grid becomes a blur — use audio cues (the rising pitch) to time your swings instead of chasing visually.

High Score & Persistence

Your best score, best combo, and games played are saved locally in your browser under the key topwebtool-whack. Nothing is sent to a server.
