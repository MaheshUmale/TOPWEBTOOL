Shape Clicker

Shapes spawn and move across the screen. Click the correct shape to rack up points before the timer runs out!

How to Play: Objective: Click only the shapes matching the target (e.g., circles). Each correct click adds 1 point; each wrong click subtracts 1 point (no going below zero). Difficulty ramps up every 15 seconds: shapes move faster and spawn more often. Survive 60 seconds and beat your high score. Controls: Mouse to click shapes, Space/ESC to pause, R to restart.
