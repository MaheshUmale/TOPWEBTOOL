About TopWebTool

Free, private, instant web tools — and the people behind them

TopWebTool is a collection of free, instant, no-signup online tools — built on one stubborn idea: a web tool should not require an account, should not slow to a crawl under a wall of trackers, and should never ship your numbers off to somebody’s server.

What we do

Money decisions — a mortgage, a refinance, a debt payoff plan, a rent-vs-buy call — start with numbers, and people deserve to run those numbers privately and instantly. Our flagship cluster is a growing set of finance and money calculators, complemented by converters, marketing and developer utilities, and a games-and-play section. Every tool runs entirely in your browser: there is no backend, no database, and nothing you type leaves your device.

How the tools work

  • 100% client-side. Calculations happen in JavaScript on your machine. Close the tab and the numbers are gone.
  • No accounts, no paywalls. Every tool is free, with no signup and no email gate.
  • Fast by architecture. Static pages on Cloudflare’s edge network — no bloated frameworks between you and the answer.
  • Transparent math. Every calculator page shows the formula it uses and a worked example, with primary sources (CFPB, Freddie Mac, IRS, and similar) cited so you can verify us.

Who is behind TopWebTool

TopWebTool is built and maintained by an independent developer, Mahesh Umale, who got tired of "free" calculator sites that demand an email address before revealing an amortization schedule. The project is developed in the open; the code that powers this site is public on GitHub, which keeps us honest.

Our editorial and accuracy standards

  • Every calculator page states its formula, assumptions, and limitations — an estimate is only useful when you can see what produced it.
  • Money content cites primary sources (Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, Federal Housing Finance Agency, Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, IRS publications) rather than blog-to-blog hearsay.
  • Pages carry a visible "last reviewed" date, and we re-check rate-sensitive pages as markets move.
  • We do not give individualized financial advice. We compute, explain, and cite — decisions belong to you and, where it matters, your licensed advisor or lender.

How TopWebTool makes money

Transparency matters more in finance than anywhere. TopWebTool is free because it is funded by (1) display advertising served through Google AdSense, and (2) occasional affiliate links to third-party financial products, always disclosed and clearly labeled. We never rank or design a calculator to steer you toward a product; where a lender would capture you as a lead, we simply give you the number and let you leave.
