How to Calculate Mortgage Payments Manually: Step-by-Step Guide

Published: August 2026 • Category: Finance & Real Estate • No Sign-Up / 100% Free / No Registration

Calculating your mortgage payment manually is an excellent way to understand where your money goes every month. The core of any mortgage calculation relies on the standard amortization formula. This formula determines the fixed monthly payment needed to pay off the principal loan balance plus interest over a specific period.

The principal and interest (P&I) formula is: M = P [ i(1+i)^n ] / [ (1+i)^n - 1 ], where M is the monthly payment, P is the principal loan amount, i is the monthly interest rate (annual interest rate divided by 12), and n is the total number of payments (loan term in years multiplied by 12). By inputting your loan details, you can manually verify your lender's figures.

In addition to principal and interest, a true monthly housing expense includes property taxes, homeowners insurance, and sometimes private mortgage insurance (PMI) or homeowners association (HOA) fees. These extra items are typically collected in an escrow account and added to your monthly P&I payment.
